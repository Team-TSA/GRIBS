# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, focus on technical evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, protect confidential information, and avoid publishing content that creates an unnecessary safety risk.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation of validation status, disclosure of private reports, and repeated disregard of repository safety procedures.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the Organization owners. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary.
