# Model assumptions and limitations

GRIBS is a lumped-parameter numerical model. Users must evaluate whether each assumption is acceptable for their application.

## Principal assumptions

1. The chamber is spatially uniform. Pressure waves, acoustic modes, and local gradients are not solved.
2. Chamber properties come from CEA HP equilibrium (or the constant-property `reference_constant` backend) tabulated over a configured pressure range and interpolated as pressure-only functions. The gas phase follows an ideal-gas state relation; non-ideal effects at high pressure are not modeled or bounded.
3. Regression follows a quasi-steady pressure law. Ignition delay, extinction, transient combustion response, and pressure-history effects are not explicitly modeled.
4. Bore and enabled end surfaces regress uniformly with a common burn depth. The outer grain surface is inhibited.
5. The erosive-burning correction does not resolve the axial flow distribution. It uses one mass flux, the total product mass flux at the aft end of the bore, G = rho_p·r·Ab/(π·Ri²) (it includes the products of the burning end faces and the condensed phase, not the igniter flow), and applies the resulting burn rate r = r0 + alpha·G^0.8/(2Ri)^0.2·exp(−beta·rho_p·r/G) uniformly to the whole burning surface, end faces included. It is therefore not a port-average model. The form is that of Lenoir–Robert with the bore diameter 2Ri as the length scale; alpha is in m^2.8·kg^−0.8·s^−0.2 (SI) and beta is dimensionless. Because G is proportional to r, the equation is solved for r at each evaluation (Newton's method); if it does not converge, the run stops with `E_EROSIVE_ROOT_NOT_CONVERGED` (exit code 4) instead of using an unconverged value.
6. Nozzle flow is quasi-one-dimensional and nominally isentropic. Losses are represented by aggregate coefficients.
7. The separation treatment is simplified and does not predict hysteresis, side loads, or unsteady asymmetric flow.
8. Blowdown temperature remains tied to the pressure-dependent chamber-property table rather than an independent energy equation.
9. With throat erosion enabled, exit area remains fixed and full nozzle-contour evolution is not modeled. If the eroding throat area becomes larger than the fixed exit area (At > Ae, compared on the radius as Rt > Rt0·√ε), the nozzle is computed with the exit as its smallest section, which is outside the model domain. The run then records the first such time in `event_times.throat_exceeds_exit_s` (an empty list when it does not occur) and reports the warning `W_THROAT_EXCEEDS_EXIT_AREA`; it continues, and the warning does not change the run verdict or the exit code.
10. Numerical self-tests check selected identities and continuity properties, not experimental validity.
11. Two-phase model B (homogeneous equilibrium): the conserved chamber mass is the total product mass (gas + condensed); condensed products move with the gas at the same velocity and temperature, and the equation of state acts on the gas phase only.
12. The condensed-phase volume is neglected against the chamber free volume.
13. The nozzle `c*` uses a constant-gamma isentropic relation. Against the CEA equilibrium reference it differs by up to about 2.3% in the tested condensed AP/Al compositions; the comparison gate G4a has not passed (see the README, "Validation status").

14. The composition is not checked for plausibility. GRIBS does not decide whether a composition is combustible, ignites or sustains combustion. The burn rate is the configured law, so the shapes of the pressure and thrust histories are set mainly by the grain geometry and the burn law. With the geometry, burn law and nozzle fixed, the normalized pressure histories of different compositions nearly overlap: the composition changes mainly their level, and their shape only slightly.
15. Condensed-phase scope is classified by the minimum gas mass fraction `Yg_min` of the thermochemistry table (code constants, not linked to validation evidence): `model_status` is classified with 0.99 / 0.65, and 0.80 only selects an additional warning. For `0.65 < Yg_min <= 0.99` the run reports `W_HIGH_CONDENSATE_MODEL_LIMITATION` (unmodelled two-phase physics). For `0.65 < Yg_min <= 0.80` it also reports `W_HIGH_CONDENSATE_G4B_UNVALIDATED`: reproduction of reported or experimental data (G4b) has not been done for this band. For `Yg_min <= 0.65` the run stops (`E_OUT_OF_VALIDATED_RANGE`).

## Property and input limitations

The chamber-property tables have a configured pressure range. With `thermochemistry.outside_range_policy = "clamp"` (used by the supplied configurations), properties outside that range are held at the nearest table limit and the run is not stopped. A run that crosses the table limit reports `W_PROPERTY_TABLE_RANGE_CROSSED` in the summary warnings. A run that spends part of its history outside the table reports `W_PROPERTY_TABLE_EXTRAPOLATED` with the fraction of sampled points outside. Golden case g2 (table 0.1-8 MPa, MEOP about 44.5 MPa) spends about 84 % of its sampled history above the table limit: it is a clamped-table regression reference, not a physically representative case, and its frozen values are intentionally unchanged. Clamping improves numerical robustness outside that interval but suppresses pressure dependence there. A run that leaves the range reports the warning `W_PROPERTY_TABLE_EXTRAPOLATED` (number and fraction of samples, policy). Extrapolation remains smooth but may be physically unreliable. Combustion coefficients, efficiencies, flow coefficients, and erosion parameters require independent evidence for the intended formulation and operating range.

## Run state

The run summary reports six run-state keys: `solver_completed`, `mass_consistency_passed`, `nominal_termination_reached`, `model_domain_passed`, `warnings_present` and `results_usable`. `results_usable` is true only when the first four are true. They are reported only for a run that completed; a run that stops with an error before the summary is made (configuration, thermochemistry, model-applicability or solver error) has no summary and no keys. They do not change `run_passed` or the exit code.

- A run that ends other than by burnout (and, with blowdown enabled, by reaching ambient pressure) reports `W_NON_NOMINAL_TERMINATION` and is not usable.
- At > Ae (`W_THROAT_EXCEEDS_EXIT_AREA`, item 9) and `E_OUT_OF_VALIDATED_RANGE` are model-domain violations; the run is not usable.
- Leaving the property-table pressure range (`W_PROPERTY_TABLE_EXTRAPOLATED`, see below) and subsonic nozzle flow during burning (`W_SUBSONIC_BURNING`; burn-phase c* indicators lose their meaning there) are warnings only and do not make the run unusable. Golden g1 and g2 leave the table range and are reported usable with this warning.
- `results_usable` is a statement about these checks only. It is not evidence that the physical model is valid (see "Validation status").

## Interpretation limits

- MEOP is the largest simulated chamber pressure, not an allowable structural pressure.
- Effective performance metrics are model outputs, not certification values.
- Mass consistency is an internal numerical diagnostic, not evidence that the physical model is correct.
- Default inputs are examples and must not be treated as design recommendations.

## Energy mode (experimental; opt-in `energy_model`; A-6 stage 2)

Energy mode is selected only by an `energy_model` section in the configuration. A configuration without that section runs the model described in the rest of this file, unchanged. The items below apply to energy mode only.

Energy mode is experimental: it is opt-in, its keys, outputs and behaviour may change in a later release without notice, and its effect on results for a real propellant has not been evaluated.

1. **Scope: synthetic fixtures only.** Energy mode is verified on synthetic fixtures: an analytic ideal-gas provider (`reference_constant`), synthetic ideal-gas providers in the tests, and two runs with the CEA provider of a golden propellant, at 3000–3245 K, above the Al2O3 melting temperature (2327 K): one checks completion, `gamma_s > 1` and the closure residuals; the other checks that the latent-heat check at t = 0 passes. In these runs `gamma_s` is 1.124–1.142 (states with liquid Al2O3). A run with the CEA provider through the melting plateau has not been tested, and the `gamma_s` of states with condensed Al2O3 has not been checked against an independent value (held item H-19); in the separate check of report 198 of T_m ± d and 2200–2500 K states, `gamma_s` is 1.149–1.191. The number of cases qualified with a real propellant is 0. No result of energy mode is compared with measured data, and no result with a condensed-phase volume is compared with the legacy model (the legacy model neglects that volume; the two are different models).
2. **Balance.** The chamber state is the total energy and the element inventories of the contents. Pressure and temperature are recovered from the energy, the free volume and the inventories. The burn-surface term is written on the solid side, in the form `mdot_burn·h_s − p·A_b·r`, with `h_s` evaluated at the far temperature of the grain (`T_grain,inf`) on the same absolute enthalpy basis as the products; heat through the burn surface is included by that evaluation and not added separately. The following are taken as stated, and the effect of each on results has not been evaluated: a quasi-steady burn surface (no storage term for ignition and tail-off), with the far end of the grain adiabatic and the web thick compared with the thermal penetration depth except just before burnout; `H0_out` taken as the enthalpy of the contents at rest, `u + p·V_free/M`, which for contents at rest is the stagnation enthalpy at the nozzle inlet plane (the kinetic energy at the inlet plane is included in it and is not added separately; contract 164 §2b-4.4 (i)); an adiabatic nozzle (`Cd` scales the mass flow only; held item H-14); no slip or hold-up between the phases (homogeneous mixture); no volume change from wall erosion upstream of the throat in `V_free` (contract 164 §2b-4.11, provisional; whether the cases have upstream erosion is not confirmed: held item H-11). In addition, the state is the total energy `E_cv` of contents at rest, and `E_cv` is treated as the internal energy (equivalent to `U_cv`): this departs from the model definition (contract 164 §2b-4.1), which allows the reduction to `U_cv` only when an upper bound of `M_port` is stated (held item H-05, not decided). The kinetic energy of the contents, the non-uniformity of the state between the contents and the nozzle inlet plane, and the unsteadiness of the flow to that plane are not modelled either (held item H-05). The effect of each has not been evaluated.
3. **Condensed phases.** Their volume is computed from constant densities and constant heat capacities given in `energy_model.condensed`. A phase transition is a plateau at the melting temperature, and the phase fraction on the plateau is a linear approximation. The effect of the constant-property assumption and of that approximation has not been evaluated.
4. **Composition.** There is one propellant source and the initial fill is the equilibrium products of that propellant (an air fill is rejected). The element fractions are computed from explicit formulas (`energy_model.propellant_formulas`), never from species names, and stay fixed. The atomic-mass table of GRIBS is not audited against the atomic masses CEA uses (held item H-17); the effect on results has not been evaluated.
5. **`gamma_s`.** The isentropic exponent of the nozzle flow is the equilibrium value `gamma_s` of the CEA TP solution (`EqSolution.gamma_s`, the definition of the legacy `gamma_s`); with `reference_constant` it is the configured `reference_gamma`. A state whose `gamma_s` is not finite or not above 1 stops the run. A frozen-flow exponent is not used. The range of `gamma_s` met by a run is recorded in the summary. The CEA provider solves every state on a new `EqSolution` (held items H-19, H-20).
6. **Two densities.** The density of the solid propellant is `Config.rho_p` (the bulk density computed from the reactants and the packing fraction) and nothing else; there is no `energy_model` key for it. It is used only in the burning mass flow `rho_p·A_b·r`. The thermodynamic density of the gas mixture, `M/V_free`, comes from the state and is used only in the outflow enthalpy `h = u + p·V_free/M`. They are different quantities and are not compared.
7. **`reference_constant` is a test convention.** The legacy `reference_constant` backend has no enthalpy. For the algebraic-limit tests, energy mode assigns it the convention `h(T_ref) = h_s(T_grain,inf)` (the adiabatic flame temperature is the reference temperature). This convention is for tests; it is not a property of any propellant, and results obtained with it must not be used as results for a propellant. In energy mode these values have no defaults: the three values (`temperature_K`, `gamma`, `molecular_weight_kg_kmol`) must be stated in `thermochemistry.reference_constant` of the configuration file; they are not taken from the defaults of the legacy loader, which fills them when `--backend reference_constant` is used on a file that does not state them (such a run is rejected).
8. **Closure residuals.** A run reports four residuals (mass, element inventory, energy, volume), each in absolute and normalised form. A run ends with exit code 0 when it completes, `gamma_s > 1` holds in every state and every normalised residual is within a threshold of `1e-6`; that is an exit-code rule, not a statement that the results are physically valid. The residuals check the self-consistency of the integration; they are not a check that the physical model is right. The threshold is provisional (held item H-09): it is a pass/fail threshold, not a claim of accuracy, and it will be fixed in the acceptance of real cases.
9. **Rejected configurations.** An igniter (`E_ENERGY_MODE_IGNITER_REJECTED`); `equilibrium_pressure_diagnostic` other than `off`; `temperature_efficiency` (`eta_T0`) other than 1; `two_phase_model.mode` other than `homogeneous_equilibrium`; an initial fill other than the products of the main propellant; a solid and a liquid of one species without the declared transition between them (`E_ENERGY_UNDECLARED_PHASE_TRANSITION`), and a transition between two solid phases (a solid-solid transition is not supported); an initial state given by the pressure only (the fill temperature is required); a non-adiabatic wall without `heat_rate_W`, or `heat_rate_W` with an adiabatic wall; `burn_surface.form` other than `a`; `condensed.volume` other than `external_density`; unknown keys; `propellant_formulas` missing, or an element symbol that is not in the atomic-mass table (symbols are case-sensitive); a reactant basis whose weights are not unique or not non-negative (found when the run starts and reported as a run failure, exit code 4); a thermochemistry backend other than `cea_python` and `reference_constant`; the backend `reference_constant` when `temperature_K`, `gamma` or `molecular_weight_kg_kmol` is not stated in the configuration file (for example `--backend reference_constant` on a file written for `cea_python`); `--selftest` or `--dump-thermo` together with an `energy_model` section (energy mode does not build the legacy thermochemistry table); `--no-cache` together with an `energy_model` section (it rebuilds the legacy chamber-property cache, which energy mode does not use); and `--allow-verification-failure` together with an `energy_model` section (the flag applies only to the legacy mass-ledger gate, which energy mode does not have; the energy closure failure is never downgraded). `--validate-config` does not make the latent-heat check (it needs the provider and the state at t = 0) or the other checks made when the run starts. The TP cache of energy mode (`thermo_cea_tp_*.json`, schema 3; earlier schemas are not read) is separate from the legacy chamber-property cache.
10. **Not produced.** An energy run does not produce the legacy figure, the legacy time history, the CEA theoretical rocket diagnostics, the equilibrium pressure diagnostic, the post-run self-test of the legacy model, the legacy mass and energy ledger or the legacy model-applicability check. The command line and the summary say so (`energy_model.legacy_outputs_not_produced`).

## Validation status

No claim of experimental validation is made by this public repository. Physical validity has not been established. Reproduction of reported or experimental data (gate G4b) has not been done, because no usable public data was found: the reported AP/Al cases do not give complete inputs, and no experimental data set or independent two-phase model was available. The reproducibility and CEA-consistency evidence and its limits are described in the README, "Validation status". Users should document solver convergence, uncertainty, independent comparisons, and experimental validation before relying on results.

Energy mode (`energy_model`) has no validation either: it is verified on synthetic fixtures only, and the number of cases qualified with a real propellant is 0 (see "Energy mode").
